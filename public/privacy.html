OrangeUni

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

OrangeUni (“we”, “us”) helps students discover, compare, and track universities. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have.

What we collect

  • Account information from Google. When you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address, and Google account ID. We don’t receive your Google password.
  • Profile details you enter. Username, display name, bio, public/private setting, country, degree level, interests, test scores (SAT, ACT, IB, GRE, GPA), budget, and preferred location.
  • Your activity in the app. Universities you shortlist, compare, or track, along with deadlines, application statuses, and notes you add.
  • Messages. Conversations you have with other students in the app.

How we use it

  • To sign you in and keep your data synced across devices.
  • To rank and recommend universities based on your profile.
  • To let other students find your public profile and message you. Private profiles are hidden from search.

We don’t sell your data, show ads, or use third-party analytics or advertising trackers.

Where it’s stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, and the web app is hosted on Vercel. University information shown in the app comes from public sources such as Wikipedia and Wikidata.

Who can see it

If your profile is public, other signed-in users can see your username, display name, and bio. Your scores, shortlist, tracker, and notes are visible only to you. Messages are visible only to the people in the conversation.

Your choices

  • Edit your profile or switch it to private at any time from the Profile tab.
  • Request a copy of your data, or deletion of your account and all associated data, by emailing us. We’ll act on deletion requests within 30 days.
  • You can revoke OrangeUni’s access to your Google account at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google user data

OrangeUni’s use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We only use your Google name, email, and account ID to create and sign in to your OrangeUni account.

Children

OrangeUni is intended for students aged 13 and older. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us and we’ll delete it.

Changes

If we change this policy we’ll update the date above. Significant changes will be announced in the app.
