OrangeUni

Find, compare, and track the right universities for you.

OrangeUni is a free university-discovery app for students. Tell it your interests, test scores, budget, and preferred countries, and it ranks thousands of universities by how well they fit you. Shortlist the ones you like, compare them side by side, track your applications through to a decision, and connect with other students applying to the same places.

Open OrangeUni

What you can do

Discover

Get a personalised ranking of universities based on your interests, academics, and budget, with Safety, Match, and Reach labels.

Shortlist

Save schools, set application deadlines, and keep notes for each one.

Compare

Put up to three universities side by side: acceptance rate, costs, test scores, and more.

Track

Follow every application from draft to decision in one place.

Connect

Find other students by username, view public profiles, and message each other.

How sign-in works

You sign in with your Google account. OrangeUni only receives your name, email address, and Google account ID, and uses them solely to create your account and keep your shortlist, tracker, and profile synced across devices. We don't sell your data or show ads. Read the full Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

Who makes OrangeUni

OrangeUni is an independent project built for students applying to university. Admission estimates are guidance based on public data, not guarantees.
