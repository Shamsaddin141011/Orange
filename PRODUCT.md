# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Domestic and international high school and undergraduate students (17-22) actively researching university options. They're overwhelmed by choice, unfamiliar with the US/UK admissions system, and anxious about fit. They use the app on mobile, often alone, trying to translate their scores and interests into a realistic shortlist.

## Product Purpose

OrangeUni removes the opacity from university matching. Instead of gut-feel or marketing copy, it shows students exactly why a school was recommended — interest overlap, SAT fit, budget, location. Success means a student walks away with a realistic shortlist they can actually act on.

## Brand Personality

Confident, warm, clear. A knowledgeable friend who's done this before — not a corporate admissions office, not a chaotic feed.

## Anti-references

- Generic fintech and banking apps: dark navy, gold accents, over-formal.
- Stock photo university websites: beige, serif fonts, institutional and cold.
- Typical dark SaaS dashboards: endless glass panels, neon accents, every tool looks the same.
- Consumer social apps: TikTok/Instagram aesthetic, chaotic, feed-first, dopamine-driven.

## Design Principles

1. **Transparency over mystique.** Show the numbers and the reasoning. Never hide the match score logic.
2. **Confident guidance, not overwhelming choice.** Prioritise the most important information; collapse everything else.
3. **Warm precision.** Data-dense but never cold. Every number earns its place through context.
4. **One action at a time.** The app guides through a clear flow: discover, evaluate, shortlist, track.
5. **Earn the trust of a stressed 17-year-old.** If it feels like homework or marketing, it has failed.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard mobile baseline: touch targets ≥44px, legible text contrast. Text sizes respect system font scale. No motion-only affordances.
